Quick commerce is mostly used by people comprising of age group from 18-29 and 30-46 respectively.

Bachelors(18-23) which are in very early stages of their lives and more inclined towards impulsive behaviour, which further promotes buying of products from limited categories snacks, beverages or fast fashion etc. Younger population leans more towards very early growing stages of life and more inclined towards trend based shopping behaviours rather than trying out products across various categories.

Newly married(24-29) tends to cover wide array of product categories as they are in much better phase of life, where they are susceptible to trying new products that aligns with their life stage. These are fairly younger families which are starting to settle down, shared decision-making, and some of the highest discretionary spending of any life stage.

Full nested families(30-46), where everything starts to change: presence of children(infant, school-age, teenage) at home produces the most dramatic and most category-specific shifts in consumer behaviour of any transition in the family life cycle. Category entry into seasonal purchases, festive purchases, infant care, pet care, household items, groceries and many more. Shifting towards more stable categories purchases, also keeping a balance between indulgence purchases. In this stage of life families are shifting towards a stable lifestyle and cutting down on unstable, impulse purchase categories like snacks, instant, frozen fast foods and trending beauty products which dominates in early stages of life. Making them more suitable for trying products from wider array of segments and categories.
