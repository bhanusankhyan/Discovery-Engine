Dairy, Fruits & Vegetables, Snacks, Beverages, Personal Care, Household Items. These categories dominate because they address immediate needs and frequent consumption patterns. 

Several factors explain why these categories dominate quick commerce orders.

Immediate Need: Quick commerce solves urgent problems, such as running out of milk or household supplies.
High Frequency Consumption: Products used daily naturally generate repeat purchases.
Impulse Buying Behaviour: Low-cost snacks and beverages are often added to carts spontaneously.
Urban Lifestyle Trends: Busy professionals prefer quick deliveries rather than visiting physical stores.

The success of quick commerce lies in its ability to fulfil immediate consumer needs. Products that sell the most on these platforms are typically everyday essentials such as groceries, snacks, beverages, personal care items, and household supplies.
