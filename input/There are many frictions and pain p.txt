There are many frictions and pain points emerge consistently in the shopping experience, some of the major frustrations are most basic and primary concerns that needs to be looked.

Quality of some products are not up to the mark, making consumers shift form one brand to another a continuous loop of experimenting. Ultimately affecting brand perception and loyalty of consumers. 

Navigating the online store became a next level dilemma as we are introducing more and more categories. A user who wants to explore will have to do the immense heavy lifting of searching through wide array of categories, have to remember what items are needed from different categories or sections that fulfil their present needs. This friction ultimately degrades the overall experience of shoppers.
