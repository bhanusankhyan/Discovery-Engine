India’s Commerce is Speeding Up

India’s E-commerce market is accelerating rapidly, with GMV surpassing ~$80 Bn and growing at ~20% annually, driven by digital adoption and rising consumer affluence. The shift to faster fulfillment is transforming consumer expectations—60+% of online consumers now say they would pay a premium for same-day delivery. India’s same-day delivery (SDD) market is expected to grow at 20–25% CAGR, reaching over $15 Bn by 2030 by 2028. Faster commerce (quick and rapid delivery models) is growing around 45+% annually, over twice the rate forecasted in China. Quick commerce services have scaled to 40+ cities across India, reflecting strong nationwide adoption and positioning the country as a fast-evolving market for “need-it-sooner” retail.


India's Structural Advantage: Why Quick Commerce Works Here

Faster commerce has faced profitability hurdles in Europe due to high costs and in Southeast Asia due to lower willingness to pay. India, similar to China, benefits from favorable cost structures, very high population densities, and growing consumer willingness to pay for speed. India’s urban density, low labor costs, convenience-led consumer culture, rising purchasing power, and fragmented offline retail base create ideal conditions for scaling faster commerce. India’s demand base is further broadening with Tier 2 and 3 cities now accounting for over 40% of online retail spending, with faster growth than metros.

The growth of non-grocery items within quick commerce GMV from ~20% to ~30% in just six months is reflecting a behavioural shift towards expecting faster delivery across everyday categories. Categories like skincare, supplements, electronics accessories and gifting are especially well-suited to rapid commerce—where urgency matters. Same-day delivery improves conversion rates and brand stickiness, offering D2C and omnichannel players a strategic edge while maintaining healthier unit economics.
